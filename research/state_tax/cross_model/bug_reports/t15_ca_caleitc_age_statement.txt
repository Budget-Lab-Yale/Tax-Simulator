federal childless age band -- a filer without qualifying children

expanded eligibility to ages 18-24 and 65+ only beginning TY2018.
TAXSIM pays a 2017 credit to this 68-year-old (v39 > 0; on our
validation sample, childless filers aged 67-73 are paid $60-$157).
