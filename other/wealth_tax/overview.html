A net-worth tax is structurally the estate tax minus death.

Estate already proves a balance-sheet tax can live in do_taxes(), aggregate with weights, book receipts, and feed distribution. A wealth tax is that — minus mortality, minus DSUE/portability, minus the gift/§2053/valuation death-time adjustments, minus the fiscal-year death-lag. The wiring path is well-trodden; the intellectual weight sits in one place only.

03

The two bathtubs

Cohort recurrences on age cells. Don’t conflate them — one is built, one is the point.

Built · the template

#1 — kg_dynamics

A capital-gains realization-timing recurrence on age cells [18,80]+tail. Provides the reusable machinery: aging matrix, per-year state, the match() applier, a frozen-mechanical vs. behavioral split, SLURM phases 1B/2B, and a calibration-provenance guard built after a 37% silent bias. Load-bearing: effective cell mortality is a wealth-weighted mean, not a cell-mean (~2.7× correction). The Bellman is realization-specific — don’t copy it.

Unbuilt · the core

#2 — wealth-dynamics

The saving-incidence channel. A share s = 1−MPC of above-baseline during-life tax is financed out of wealth, compounds at r_total, and drains into the estate base at death — opening income/CG → wealth → estate. Without it, a wealth tax assumes a fixed stock and can never erode the future base it logically should. The wealth-tax ΔT is exactly the during-life capital ΔT this is built to consume.

⚠ Design in flux The plan says static inflow / behavioral module; the latest session overturned both → conventional ΔT / mechanical application, and found the “consistency is automatic” claim wrong (replace with a mass-conservation invariant). Reconcile before wiring.

05

Interactions & blind spots

Wealth is the most-coupled base on the model — it lands on the same balance sheet as three other things.

Double-tax-at-death

In the death year both taxes hit the same stock. Paying the wealth tax should shrink the future estate base — but with the fixed-stock model it never does. Only the 2nd bathtub closes the loop; without it, long-run bases are overstated.

Valuation-bridge hazard

Don’t reuse estate_valuation_params.yaml. Sharing r/rho_pt couples the bases (an estate recalibration silently moves wealth), and those factors were fit to death-time §2031 returns — a different regime. Reuse the data + the cluster idea, not the bridge.

Donor clones — worse here

The estate cap is a death-weight cap; clones “barely die” out of the base. An annual tax weights living clones every year, exposing the full duplicated top-tail mass exactly where a wealth tax concentrates. Needs a fresh living-weight cap.

MTR for a stock

A next-dollar wealth MTR does exist (cleaner than income’s). The real issue: the response is base-erosion on stocks, which apply_mtr_elasticity (flow-built) doesn’t support — and WTS’s elasticities (−7/−17) imply near-total disappearance. Recalibrate; don’t copy.

Tax-Data artifacts

The wages residual contaminates the bathtub inflow; the sample-universe fix is more critical at the top tail (forces pct_sample=1); age1 is top-coded at 80 — the top age bin must be 80+, exactly where wealth concentrates.

Static vs. conventional

Estate books FY t+1; wealth must book in-year. The bathtub’s static-inflow approximation is flagged “may not be second-order” at wealth-tax magnitudes — now compounded by the overturn to a conventional inflow.

06

Migration path

P0–P2 deliver a usable static + behavioral tax on their own. P3 is what makes it credible.

P0

Decide the base

Validate top-tail net worth on the living population. Decide economic net worth vs. a reported-valuation bridge — and that any wealth valuation factors live in a new frozen file, never shared with estate.

gates: economic vs reported · is a measurement file needed at all
P1

Static first-class tax

calc_wealth() + wealth.yaml + detail_vars + get_wealth_totals + the calc_receipts extension (pure level, FY=CY) + the SLURM Phase 3a mirror. Low-risk: estate is an exact template.

gates: filing-status doubling · payment timing · VAT refuse-vs-guard
P2

Within-year avoidance

do_wealth_avoidance() base-erosion module with recalibrated elasticities + a provenance guard; a living-weight donor-clone cap; an optional net_worth MTR carve-out.

gates: authoritative elasticities · stock-MTR vs ATR anchor
P3

Dynamic saving incidence

The credibility phase. Part A: build the 2nd bathtub (reuse kg scaffolding; SLURM 2B-sibling, 2C afterok both). Part B: extend the during-life inflow to add liab_wealth — excluding death-time legs, from raw components. Depends on the bathtub landing first.

gates: reconcile plan-vs-overturned design · mass-conservation invariant · clamp binding
P4

Deprecate the standalone

Reconstitute its only live composition (the hand-rolled wealth+income surtax in why_not_analysis.R) as one first-class scenario; port the wealth-percentile distribution flavor; document why standalone revenue won’t reconcile; archive the repo.

gates: own distribution tier vs folded · migrate vs drop SZZ series
