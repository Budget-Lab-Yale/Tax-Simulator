Finding — a real income-ranking effect, opposite the plan's initial guess. Under an income ranking, the capital-income base loads ~50% of the corporate tax onto the income-top 1% (they mechanically hold ~half of all capital income), whereas the equity stock base spreads it to ~29% — because equity stock includes DC / retirement (0.55 weight) held more broadly than top incomes. The plan's "top-1% corporate ETR rises moving capital income → equity/supernormal" holds only under a wealth ranking, which this file doesn't emit. The 28.8% equity/supernormal share sits right in the SZ / TPC ~30–35% ballpark.
