Wealth bathtub · sensitivity bounds

How much do s and M move a capital-gains reform?

Bounding the wealth saving-financing channel on a +5pp top capital-gains rate under carryover basis at death. The saving share s is the first-order knob; the within-age wealth-mobility operator M is second-order. Across the full plausible range the conventional 10-year estimate sits at $546B (no channel) falling to $524B at the s=1 extreme — an envelope of about 22B, of which M contributes at most ~$2B.

Bottom line

For a capital-income reform, s dominates: financing the tax hike out of wealth (rather than consumption) erodes the future capital-income and estate bases, and the cross-base cost scales almost linearly — from −$5.6B at s=0.25 to −$21.9B at s=1 (about −$22B per unit of s). At a central s=0.5 the channel costs −$11.1B over ten years (~2% of the estimate).

M is second-order. Swapping full persistence (identity) for extreme diffusion (uniform 1/n) moves the total by at most ~$2B over ten years (<0.4%) — and even that is mostly reallocation, not magnitude: diffusion shifts leakage off the threshold'd estate base (persistence concentrates the drain on estate-taxable wealth) and onto payroll (it spreads onto working-age pass-through owners), the two nearly cancelling. At realistic near-identity mobility, M is negligible.

First order: the saving share s

At s=0 the wealth channel is dormant and the reform is the clean law-plus-realization estimate: $546B over ten years, essentially all income tax. Turning the channel on diverts a share s of the net during-life tax increase into wealth decumulation, which compounds and shrinks the bases that later get taxed.

Second order: the mobility operator M

M re-grids the accumulated wealth deficit across within-age net-worth percentiles each year. To bound it we ran the identical reform twice: under identity (full persistence — a record's wealth rank never moves; the v1 default, and where the truth sits) and under uniform 1/n (extreme diffusion — ranks fully re-drawn every year). The two estimates and their difference are below. At s=0 they are identical — the channel is dormant, so M cannot bite until s>0.

What M changes is where the leakage lands, not its size. Under identity the drain stays on the high-net-worth records that paid the tax, so leakage concentrates in income and the threshold'd estate base. Under diffusion it spreads onto working-age pass-through / self-employment owners, shifting leakage toward payroll and away from estate. The two nearly cancel: the total moves by at most ~$2B over ten years.

Mechanism & the clamp caveat

The drained wealth is small against the stock: at s=0.5 the cumulative drain reaches $348B by 2036 against a $290T net-worth base (~0.12%), rising to $688B at s=1. The aggregate drain is conserved across M (the operator is doubly-stochastic) — M only redistributes it.

Clamp caveat. The per-record haircut is capped at |f| ≤ fmax = 0.9. Under identity it almost never binds (≤49 records, 0.06M people at s=1). Under uniform it binds heavily — 2036 records (3.3M people) at s=1 — because diffusion dumps deficit onto low-net-worth cells that cannot absorb it. So the high-s uniform bound is partly clamp-shaped, not pure diffusion; the realistic (near-identity) case carries negligible clamping. This reinforces that M is second-order for plausible mobility.

Method

  • Reform. Top preferred rate 0.20→0.25 (+5pp) with carryover basis at death for all asset classes (kg_death_regime_* = 1); behavioral realization via kg_dynamics/turnover. Baseline = current law.
  • Grid. s ∈ {0, .25, .5, .75, 1} at M = identity; s ∈ {.25, .5, .75, 1} at M = uniform (s=0 is channel-dormant, hence M-independent). Two serialized full-sample pipelines (M is read live at the wealth pre-pass, so they cannot overlap).
  • Window. 10 fiscal years FY2027–2036 (policy effective CY2026, receipts FY-booked with a filing-season t+1 lag; estate booked death-year+1). Sim run one year past the window.
  • What's varied — and what isn't. Only s and M. s is flat across ages and symmetric (v1); calibration of kg realization (psi/planned_share) is held fixed. Static estimates are M- and s-invariant by construction; the interaction is conventional-only.
  • Conservative s=1. The fmax clamp means s=1 understates the unconstrained drain — treat it as a soft upper bound.
