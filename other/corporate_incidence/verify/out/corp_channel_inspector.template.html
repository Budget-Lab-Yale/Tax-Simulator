The machine

One wedge in, five consequences out

The input is a single series from Off-Model-Estimates (OME) — the interface that carries revenue effects estimated outside the microsimulation as bare CSVs of receipts deltas. The channel reads the corporate column, asserts a fail-closed declaration (corporate_meta.yaml: gross-of-offset, rate-type), and turns it into record-level transformations. Everything downstream is a consequence the calculator prices on its own.

1 · INPUT

OME corporate wedge w_t

Receipts delta, $B by year. Gate: metadata + seesaw guard, else channel off.

2 · PATHS

Analytic φ_t and μ_t

Flow factor (payout cut) and perfect-foresight equity markdown, from π_t = gdp_corp − rev_corp, vintaging η(t), κ-split.

3 · RECORDS

Applier transforms the frame

Dividends/interest/rent/pt scaled; exposed value.* marked down; kg gains adjusted; retirement draws follow their balances.

4 · CALCULATOR

Taxes reprice

IIT, NIIT, estate and wealth tax all recompute on the shocked frame. No hand-coded offset anywhere.

5 · RECEIPTS

Endogenous offset materializes

Corporate line books the wedge; the individual-side loss shows up in ordinary conventional receipts deltas.

Wealth bathtub branch. A share s of the net after-tax cash-flow shock F = ΔT⁰ − ΔY_exog is financed out of wealth, compounds, and drains into the estate base at death.
kg_dynamics branch. In kg runs the price margin enters as a gain-state debit (μ_t × exposed value ≈ $1.2–1.5T/yr here) and the quantity margin as a post-behavior φ step.

Step 2 · Paths

Two numbers carry the whole shock

The flow factor φ_t is the proportional cut to after-tax distributions — dividends fall by ω_div·φ_t immediately (“dividends track the current-year statute”). The markdown μ_t is the perfect-foresight equity price discount: the PV of remaining flow hits. Under the permanent hike μ starts at 2.7% and decays slowly toward the rent-share floor as capital migrates (η(t) ramp); under the sunset it is the small annuity share of the window and hits exactly zero at expiry — holders during the window earn low dividends plus recovery appreciation summing to r.

Step 3 · Flows

Who loses cash income, line by line

The applier scales each external-income line (the D16 contract) and accumulates the applied dollars into corp_dY_exog. Dividends dominate at these parameters: interest ramps in only as old paper rolls over, rent and the pass-through capital slice arrive with the migration ramp η(t). Realized capital gains and retirement distributions also fall, but they are internal conversions — their resource loss is already booked in the markdown, so only their tax consequence flows through (that distinction drives the bathtub sign below).

Steps 3–4 · Stocks

The balance-sheet event reaches the estate tax

The markdown reprices exposed value.* holdings — a $1.8–1.9T household markdown position — and net_worth is recomputed, so calc_estate sees smaller gross estates. Expected estate tax falls ≈ $0.7–0.9B per year under the permanent hike. Under the sunset, the markdown leg vanishes at expiry: estates snap back to baseline — unless the wealth bathtub is on, in which case the during-window dissaving persists and compounds (D17). That persistent green line is the plan's “sunsets are not free” result.

Branch · Bathtub

The forcing sign is a composition story

The bathtub finances a share s = 0.5 of the net after-tax cash-flow shock F = ΔT⁰ − ΔY_exog out of wealth. Under the sunset, the flow leg dominates (μ is small, so internal-conversion rebates are small): F is positive, households genuinely dissave, and the accumulated deficit keeps compounding after expiry. Under the permanent hike, the big markdown makes realized-gain and retirement tax rebates large — and those are rebates on losses already booked in the markdown — so F turns mildly negative: households bank ≈ $17B of rebates by 2033, against the $1.9T markdown. Same rule, opposite signs, both correct.

Permanent + s = 0.5 — banked rebates

$B: yearly forcing F (bars) and accumulated position P (line); P < 0 = wealth above baseline

Sunset + s = 0.5 — genuine dissaving

$B: forcing stops at expiry (2031) but the deficit persists and compounds

Why this is not a sign bug. A dollar of lost dividends enters the forcing net of tax (a loss). A dollar of “lost” realized gains does not — the loss already lives in the marked-down balance sheet; only its tax rebate is cash. The formal model records exactly this corollary (P9) so it wouldn't later be “fixed.” The verification suite checks the invariant that is actually theorems: F exceeds the tax-only forcing by exactly |ΔY_exog|, and the state matches an independent recomputation to the cent.

Sweeps

The knobs move the markdown the way the theory says

σ_N is the normal-return share of the wedge (what can migrate); κ is the C-corp share of the normal-capital stock (what stays on corporate flows after migration). Both are second-order for flows and first-order for the markdown path. The priced-as-permanent corner disbelieves the legislated sunset for pricing only — the markdown stays near the permanent level through the window instead of decaying to zero.
