# JR-only side note — +2pp preferred-rate option (NOT in the Yagan memo)

Per the plan, an internal comparison of our +2pp experiment against CBO's
Dec-2024 informal option to raise capital-gains rates. Kept out of the memo
because the windows and effective dates don't line up.

## Our number (form_memo vintages, full sample, Tax-Data 2026070814)

+2pp across all preferred brackets (0/15/20 → 2/17/22), effective 2027,
FY2027–2036, scenario − baseline, full behavioral stack:

| form | 10-yr static | 10-yr conventional |
|---|---:|---:|
| levels (semi) | $344.1B | **$142.7B** |
| net-of-tax | $344.1B | **$146.1B** |

The two forms bracket ~$143–146B conventional — essentially the same this close
to the current rate.

## Caveat before setting it beside CBO

Do NOT quote these as directly comparable to CBO's line without adjusting for:
- **Window:** ours is FY2027–2036; CBO's Dec-2024 option is FY2025–2034.
- **Effective date:** ours enacts 2027; CBO's 2026.
- **Convention:** ours is the conventional (behavioral) estimate with the full
  dials stack (kg realization + entity-shifting + evasion + estate); confirm
  what elasticity/behavioral convention CBO's option uses before comparing.

The CBO Dec-2024 option figure itself is not reproduced here — look it up in
"Options for Reducing the Deficit, 2025 to 2034" and shift our window/effective
date onto its basis before drawing the comparison.
