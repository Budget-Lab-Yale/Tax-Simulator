The three views, one story. Marginal revenue (left) stays positive but collapses toward zero — the Laffer curve bending to a plateau (still fractionally rising at 45%, so the literal peak is just past the grid). The semi-elasticity s = −dlnB/dτ (middle) is roughly flat — the entropy form gives a constant per-point response by construction; step-up ≈ 2.5 recovers the −2.52 calibration anchor, and deemed sits at ≈1.3–1.4 (half as elastic — a chunk realizes mechanically at death). The log-log elasticity ε = −dlnB/dlnτ = s·τ (right) is the one that answers "where's the peak": revenue maxes where ε = 1. All three regimes climb toward the dashed line but haven't crossed it in-grid; deemed is furthest below, so its revenue-maximizing rate is the highest — closing the death-regime escape lets you tax gains harder.

Definitions. Between adjacent +5pp steps, within regime; base = realized long-term gains; τ = realization-weighted MTR (below the statutory top rate, which is why the statutory peak sits above 1/η ≈ 40%). Source kg_v3_revmax / kg_dynamics_summary.csv.
