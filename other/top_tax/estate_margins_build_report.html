TL;DR. Both margins are built, unit-tested, and verified end-to-end on full-sample SLURM runs. The estate tax now (a) discounts the kg death value — an estate-taxable cell prices dying with the gain at τ·(1−e), so estate-only reforms move realization behavior for the first time — and (b) responds to its own rate, via the exact Kopczuk–Slemrod net-of-tax power form on the reported base. The crown finding from the verification runs: under a pure 2009-parameters estate reform, realizations rise in all 63 age cells (previously exactly zero response), and current-law estate exposure of the top gain decile is ~18% of the marginal death-tax value — lock-in at the top was being overstated by that much. Your 2026-07-12 rulings are executed: σ = 0.16 is shipped and confirmed (ETI 0.2508 on the 0.25 target; §7), the η check ran and found a +3.7% drift — re-pin to ≈2.4901 recommended but left for the full revalidation loop (§6, F2) — and the F1 convention is accepted as disclosed (§6).

1What was built

Shared primitive — mtr_estate via a base-side bump

calc_estate() gains an optional input estate_base_bump (default 0) inside the estate_base pmax, and calc_mtrs() learns var = 'estate': it remaps the perturbation to that input, reprices the estate branch (calc_estate_flag), and differences the DSUE-blended expected liability against same-frame actuals. Two guards ride the call: a length assert on the actuals and a bumped-vs-actual estate_p_dsue identity, which doubles as the row-order guard (a shuffled frame fails loudly instead of producing garbage). One perturbation yields two columns in every static/conventional detail file:

  • mtr_estate — the raw, un-switched marginal estate rate. Positive by construction, conditional-on-death (no mortality), 0 below the exemption. Consumed by part (b).
  • mtr_estate_ded = estate.income_tax_ded × mtr_estate — the switch-gated rate. Consumed by part (a), so the deductibility interaction vanishes when a reform turns the §2053-style deduction off while the raw rate is untouched.

Scenario legs of kg runs get a run.R guarantee fallback (the wealth-carry mtr_net_worth pattern, no law gate since estate law is always active); baseline legs must register estate in mtr_vars — all 59 kg/wealth runscripts were swept and patched, baseline rows included.

Part (a) — leg-paired estate exposure in the kg Bellman and τeq

A new gain-weighted cell aggregator (kg_dyn_aggregate_cell_estate, clamped to [0,1]) turns record-level mtr_estate_ded into per-(age, year) exposure matrices — one per leg: e_B from baseline static detail, e_S from reform static detail. They enter at exactly two points:

  • Bellman death value: F = (1 − c_φ,eff) · τ · (1 − e). Pass 1 gets e_B, Pass 2 gets e_S. Inert under deemed (F is already 0), hardest-biting under step-up, scales carryover.
  • τeq death-realize term in the shared flow helper kg_dyn_tau_eq_flow: m·(1−p_char)·realize·τ·(1−e). The route (carryover-deferral) term, the during-life r_S·τ term, and the wealth-carry h term stay undiscounted — rationale comments in place, and the no-double-count claim is demonstrated by the one-record accounting test, not asserted.

State files carry estate_e_B/estate_e_S diagnostic columns, and each kg scenario-year writes a kg_estate_exposure_diag_{t}.csv comparing the cell aggregate against the record-level gain-weighted distribution (zero-exposure share, near-top-rate share, gain-decile profile, the 80+ donor-clone cell).

Part (b) — reported-estate own-rate response

In wealth/avoidance.R, the exact KS net-of-tax power form on the change in the un-switched estate price:

retained = ((1 − τ_S) / (1 − τ_B)) ^ ESTATE_REPORT_EPS      # 0.16, band 0.10–0.22, env-overridable
estate_concealed_frac = 1 − (1 − union) · retained          # union = wealth concealment ∪ income evasion

It stacks as the third driver of the one hidden ledger (the plan's "two drivers" predated the evasion cross-base commit), full strength (no CHI — own-base), keys strictly off the τ change (level avoidance is already in the frozen valuation bridge), and honors the firewall: estate_base only; estate_distributable and value.* byte-invariant. Rate cuts are symmetric (retained > 1 surfaces previously unreported estate). A full ESTATE_AVOID_PROVENANCE header block documents sources, caveats, and the denominator convention.

6Flags for your review

F1 — KS-on-gross denominator convention near the exemption kink (magnitude now measured: moderate). The plan applies estate_concealed_frac to reported_gross, while the KS elasticity is of the reported taxable estate — an overstatement that is worst for newly taxable records just over a lowered exemption, where a 9%-of-gross haircut can wipe out much of a thin taxable band. The pure-channel run puts the aggregate at 12.9% of the static estate delta given back under a 2009-parameters reform — defensible for ε = 0.16, so I recommend keeping the plan's convention and disclosing it with the ε band. If you ever want it tighter, the fix is one line: apply the response to estate_base (pre gift add-back) instead of gross, at the cost of breaking symmetry with the wealth-concealment convention.

F2 — η anchor re-check: RUN (author-directed), drift confirmed, re-pin recommended. (1−e_B) discounts the death value under current law, so the calibrated long-run realization elasticity drifts. Measured on a fresh 32-year post-build baseline (eta_estate_check_v1; harness other/kg_model_tests/eta_estate_check.R, job 18004072): the calibrator's long-run moment at η = 2.3992 is −2.24 without the estate term vs −2.15 with it — a 3.7% weakening; the η that restores the target with the estate term live is 2.4901 (+3.8%). Grid-wide gain-weighted current-law exposure is 0.075 (max cell 0.177). Recommendation: re-pin KG_DYN_DEFAULT_ETA to ≈2.49 — not done here, because the constant change formally requires the spec-v3 full-sim revalidation loop (dilution re-measurement + regression) and flags pre-change kg vintages as non-comparable. The internal-moment measurement is the whole input that loop needs.

F3 — every pre-build baseline vintage is dead for kg and wealth-behavior scenarios. read_mtr hard-stops on baseline detail lacking mtr_estate_ded, and the avoidance guard stops on missing mtr_estate. Old baseline_vintage pointers for these scenario classes will fail loudly with a "re-run the baseline static pass" message. Intentional, per plan.
