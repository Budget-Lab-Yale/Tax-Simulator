The atlas

Each rate lever, on its own

The four rate levers scored standalone at a single intensity, revenue-matched by hand so they compare on equal footing. "Survives" is conventional as a share of static. Revenue is 10-year cumulative, in the units selected up top.

This is a layout mock. Every number is synthetic — generated client-side from a plausible overlap model so the controls behave. Real figures come from the 128-combo factorial (7 binary switches; effective 2027; 10-yr window 2027–2036) once the batch completes.

Revenue can be shown as % of GDP (10-yr revenue ÷ 10-yr cumulative nominal GDP, ≈ $360T) or in dollars (10-yr cumulative), via the toggle up top. Switches: top ordinary +5pp · capital gains → 39.6% · corporate → 28% · wealth 1% > $50M · deemed realization at death · estate $5M / 45% · repeal QBI.

On "capital gains & dividends." It is not a separate receipt — the model books revenue into seven destinations (income tax, payroll, corporate, estate, wealth, VAT, other). It is the preferential-rate slice of the individual income tax (liab_pref + §1250 + collectibles, from totals/1040.csv, both legs), carved out for legibility in the destination ledger only (the effective-rate chart keeps income tax lumped, since the distribution pipeline is static-sourced and doesn't decompose it per percentile); ordinary income tax is the residual, so credits park in the ordinary bucket. Qualified dividends ride with long-term gains; gains taxed at death under the deemed regime join this head.
