Corporate Tax Distribution and ETR Review

Review of the recent top-tax corporate-only run, its distribution-table incidence allocation, and its treatment in effective tax rates.

Prepared July 11, 2026 · Branch: wealth · Run vintage: top_tax_dials_30y_v1

Bottom line: Corporate tax is present in both outputs. It is hard to see because the standard distribution file bundles it into the broadest tax tier, while the ETR file exposes it only in the combined wealth_cit_vat tier. The ETR denominator is also grossed up by allocated corporate tax, muting the change in the total reported ETR.

Recent Runs

The relevant completed 30-year batch is:

/nfs/roberts/scratch/pi_nrs36/jar335/model_data/Tax-Simulator/v1/top_tax_dials_30y_v1

It contains 199 scenarios covering 2026–2057. The corporate-only scenario is s_corp_r28, representing a 28% corporate rate beginning in 2027. The current runscript has subsequently moved to a 2027 simulation start under the surprise-enactment convention.

The ETR output now includes two reform legs:

  • Static: law-only tax liability, or the amount asked.
  • Conventional: liability collected after modeled income and behavioral effects.

Distribution Allocation

The model uses a stock-based Harberger allocation rather than allocating corporate tax solely by observed capital income.

  • The default normal-return share is sigma_N = 0.375.
  • The supernormal-return portion is allocated by C-corporate-equity exposure.
  • The normal-return portion is split 50% to labor and 50% to broad net capital.
  • Capital portions receive a 40% foreign-owner haircut; labor does not.
  • Owner-occupied housing enters broad capital at 70% of net home equity, excluding the estimated land share.

Under these assumptions, approximately 67.5% of the national corporate revenue change is assigned to US households:

(1 - .375) × .60 + .375 × .50 + .375 × .50 × .60 = 67.5%

The remaining 32.5% is principally foreign-borne. Therefore, the household distribution is intentionally smaller than the national corporate revenue score.

Effective Tax Rates

The ETR file allocates both the baseline level of corporate receipts and the reform delta. It reports three incidence conventions:

  • equity_supernormal: headline stock-based convention.
  • capital_income: agency-style reconciliation using capital income.
  • uniform_networth: allocation across net worth.

In 2027, allocated baseline corporate tax is approximately $283.0B and allocated reform corporate tax is approximately $315.9B, a $32.94B increase. The corporate tax is therefore present and reconciles to the distribution result.

The total ETR rises less than the corporate component because expanded and Haig-Simons income add allocated corporate tax to both the numerator and the pre-tax-income denominator. The conventional leg is smaller still because modeled income, realization, and individual-income-tax offsets reduce the amount collected.

Assessment

  • This is primarily a presentation problem, not a corporate-tax omission.
  • The burden is strongly concentrated toward the top, although the labor leg and broad stock base make it less concentrated than a pure capital-income allocation.
  • The corporate-income gross-up mechanically mutes the change in total ETR.
  • The combined wealth_cit_vat tier makes the corporate component difficult to identify without inspecting the component columns.
  • Rounded total-ETR displays in the atlas can make a 0.1–0.2 percentage-point corporate effect appear negligible.
