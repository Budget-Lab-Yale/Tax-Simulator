## Income concentration measurement debate

**PSZ vs Auten-Splinter — top 1% share of PRE-TAX national income:**
- **Piketty-Saez-Zucman (WID.world):** **2022 = 20.73%** (latest real obs); 2019 = 19.04%.
- **Auten-Splinter:** JPE 2024 **2019 = 13.79%**; 2025 update (thru 2022) **2022 = 15.51%** (davidsplinter.com/AS-Update-2025.pdf).
- **Gap: ~5.2 pp (2022).** Both agree closely on *fiscal* (tax-return) income (AS 2019 fiscal top-1%: 19.43%); the entire gap is the allocation of income that never appears on tax returns.
- **Why (one sentence):** starting from the same tax data and national-income total, PSZ allocate missing/untaxed income (underreported business income, tax-exempt capital income, retained profits) disproportionately to the top; Auten-Splinter spread it far more evenly. Sources: PSZ Comment Sept 2024 (gabriel-zucman.eu/files/PSZ2024.pdf); AS reply (davidsplinter.com/AutenSplinter-2024-ReplyToPSZ.pdf).

**CBO, "The Distribution of Household Income, 2022"** (pub. 62300, Jan 2026): top 1% share before transfers/taxes **17.8%**; after **14%**; top 1% **average federal tax rate 31.2%**; share of federal taxes 27.3%. (2021 companion: top 1% ETR 29.8%, all households 17.4%.)

**Aggregate unrealized (Haig-Simons) gains at the top:**
- **Americans For Tax Fairness (Jan 2024, Fed SCF/DFA, 2022):** total US unrealized gains ≈ **$48T**; **top 1% = $21.2T (44%)**; billionaires + centi-millionaires (≥$100M, ~64,000 households) = **$8.5T (18%)**; unrealized gains = 56% of their wealth vs 24% average.
- **Saez-Zucman:** families >$50M hold **~$8T** unrealized.
- "top 1% ≈ $23T" / "top 0.1% ≈ $13.7T" figures [UNVERIFIED].
- **Forbes 400 (2025 edition, Sept 2025): aggregate $6.6T (record); entry threshold $3.8B.** 2026 edition unpublished.

## Effective tax rates on the top

- **White House CEA (Sept 2021), Leiserson & Yagan: 8.2%** average federal *individual income tax* rate for the wealthiest 400 families, 2010–2018 (range 6–12%). **Method:** Haig-Simons denominator including unrealized gains — the denominator, not the numerator, drives the 8.2%.
- **IRS SOI top-400** (realized/AGI denominator; last published TY2014): ETR range ~17–30%; low **16.6% in 2007**; **22.9% in 2013** (post-ATRA + NIIT).
- **CBO top 1% all-federal: 29.8% (2021) / 31.2% (2022)**; all households 17.4%. **Top 0.1% individual income tax 2021: ~23% (Treasury) – 25% (TPC)**; top 0.1% all-federal ~33%. TPC long series: top 0.01% ETR ~50% (1945) → ~25% (2015).
- **The core tension: ~23–30% (realized denominator) vs 8.2% (accrual denominator) — entirely a denominator choice.**

## Revenue option benchmarks (10-year scores)

*CBO Budget Options & Greenbook = conventional scoring. CBO options FY2025–2034; CRFB reproductions rescale to FY2026–2035 (~15% higher).*

**CBO, Options for Reducing the Deficit: 2025-2034 (Dec 2024), pub. 60557:**
- **All seven ordinary rates +1pp = ~$1.2T/10yr** (CRFB FY26–35; ~$1.0T CBO FY25–34).
- **Cap gains/dividends +2pp = $110B/10yr.** Revenue-maximizing LT rate under current law ≈ **~28%** (TPC; JCT/Treasury similar); realization elasticity ≈ −0.72 (CRS R41364).
- **Corporate +1pp (21→22%) = $140B/10yr**; roughly linear ⇒ 21→28% ≈ ~$1T.
- **SS taxable max:** to 90% of covered earnings ≈ ~$800B; 12.4% on earnings >$250k (no benefit credit) ≈ ~$1.6T; full elimination (with credit) ≈ $3.2T (Trustees via CRFB) [CBO exact totals UNVERIFIED].
- **Tax gains at death: ~$536B/10yr (CBO FY25–34)**; **carryover basis ~$230B**.

**Treasury Greenbook / AFP era:**
- **AFP 39.6% on gains >$1M + deemed realization at death (scored jointly):** FY2022 Greenbook **$322B**; FY2025 Greenbook **$288.5B** (+ ~$97.2B estate/gift); Tax Foundation GE score of same: $221.0B.
- **Step-up tax expenditure (JCT, JCX-48-24): ~$72.5B in FY2026; $379.3B over FY2025–2029.**

**Wealth taxes:** Warren 2%/3% (2019): **Saez-Zucman ~$2.75T/10yr**. Warren 2%/6% (2020): **S-Z ~$3.75T | PWBM $2.3–2.7T | Summers-Sarin ~40% of S-Z**. PWBM dynamic: economy 0.9–2.1% smaller by 2050. Warren Ultra-Millionaire Act reintroduced 2026 (S-Z score ~$6.2T/10yr [note inconsistency with older scores]).

**Billionaire/MTM:** **Biden Billionaire Minimum Income Tax (25% incl. unrealized, >$100M): Treasury FY2025 ~$502.6B/10yr.** **Wyden Billionaires Income Tax: JCT preliminary $557.2B/10yr** (Nov 2021, front-loaded); reintroduced Sept 2025.

**The "limits" argument + counterpoints:**
- **Manhattan Institute / Riedl, "The Limits of Taxing the Rich":** aggressive tax-the-rich agenda ≈ **at most ~2.1% of GDP static (~$7T/decade with feedback)** vs ≥5% of GDP needed to stabilize debt; liquidating all billionaire wealth funds government ~9 months.
- **CRFB:** CBO revenue menu could raise "well over $12T" — tax-the-rich necessary but insufficient.
- **Counterpoint — Saez-Zucman:** avoidance is an enforcement CHOICE, not a constant; *Triumph of Injustice* package raises far more. (Directly resonant with this report's "elasticity is a policy choice" thesis — from the other side.)

## Behavioral parameters used by scorekeepers

- **Realization elasticities:** Dowd-McClelland-Muthitacharoen (2015 NTJ): **permanent ≈ −0.72, transitory ≈ −1.2.** JCT permanent ≈ −0.68/−0.79; Treasury OTA ≈ −0.72. Consensus ~−0.7 / >|−1| transitory. (Budget Lab preferred: −0.6 permanent / −1.2 short-run — inside the range.)
- **ETI:** Saez-Slemrod-Giertz (2012): best 0.12–0.40, **central 0.25**, higher at top. Gruber-Saez 0.40 taxable / 0.12 broad. Newer: Neisser meta ~0.54/0.67; Kumar-Liang 0.46–1.4 top.
- **Revenue-maximizing CG rate:** **Agersnap-Zidar (2021 AER:I): 38–47%** long-run (revenue elasticity −0.3 to −0.5); JCT/TPC current-law convention ~28–32%. Step-up repeal raises the revenue-max rate (no agency point estimate under repeal — exactly the gap this model fills). Sarin-Summers-Zidar-Zwick (2022): conventional scores overstate realization responses.

## Recent discourse hooks (2025–2026)

- **OBBBA distribution:** CBO pub. 61367 (resources): bottom decile −~$1,200/yr, top decile +~$13,600/yr. TPC: ~60% of tax benefit to top quintile; top 0.1% cut ~$300k (3.3% of ATI) in 2026; **~17% of the top 1% pay MORE** (pass-through SALT limits, AMT expansion). Yale Budget Lab (Jul 2025): top 1% little relief beyond TCJA extension; combined OBBBA+tariffs bottom 10% ≈ −6.5%.
- **Top rate: 37% permanent (NOT 39.6%)** — OBBBA cancelled the reversion. SALT cap $40k (2025), phases down >$500k MAGI, **reverts to $10k in 2030**.
- **Estate/gift exemption 2026 = $15M/person ($30M/couple), permanent, indexed from 2027 — draft's $15M VERIFIED.**
- **Live proposals:** Wyden MTM reintroduced Sept 2025 (>$500B claim); Warren Ultra-Millionaire 2026; **California Nov 2026 ballot: one-time 5% on net worth >$1B** (~$100B/5yr, ~200 households; Saez-Zucman op-ed support; Galle-Gamage-Saez-Shanske report Feb 2026). OBBBA left step-up fully intact. *Moore v. United States* (2024) left direct taxation of unrealized gains an open constitutional question.
- **NIIT (3.8%) and 0.9% Additional Medicare thresholds frozen since 2013** (real value eroded ~38%) — quiet bracket creep at the top.
