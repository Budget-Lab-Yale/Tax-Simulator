The flip lives in the capital-gains row. In Figure 3 the capital-gains fill collects $227B against a $550B ask — $323B leaks as investors defer under step-up — and corporate leaks a little too. In Figure 4 the same rate increase, now paired with taxation at death, collects $714B — $164B more than its ask: deferral no longer works, so realizations come forward. The deemed tax also books into the ordinary-income row (+$151B over its ask). Both packages nick the estate and corporate bases (drains, left of $0). Source: Tax-Simulator dials-v2 surrogate — static ask (sh) and conventional (ch) revenue by head, evaluated at each package; interaction shown as the per-base residual. Interpolated within the tool's validated bound; corporate 25% is the linear 21→28 interpolation.

On "do we need another run": no. These are the real numbers from the surrogate already behind the interactive — static-by-base, conventional-by-base, and per-policy, with the interaction as a per-base residual.
