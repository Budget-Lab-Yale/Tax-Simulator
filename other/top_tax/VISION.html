1. The research question

How much additional revenue is actually available at the top of the income and wealth distribution?

Progressive tax agendas propose raising revenue from the same small group of people through several statutes at once: top ordinary rates, capital gains rates, the treatment of gains at death, the estate tax, a wealth tax, the corporate tax, QBI repeal, NIIT expansion. Scoring each proposal in isolation and summing overstates the combined capacity, because the bases overlap mechanically (the same dollar of wealth is capital income during life, estate at death, wealth-tax base in between, and corporate profit upstream) and behaviorally (avoidance re-routes income between bases; evasion removes it).

The model now closes these loops on actual record-level balance sheets rather than through assumed elasticities: realization behavior that responds to the death regime (kg Bellman), a saving-financing channel that drains during-life taxes out of future capital-income/estate/wealth bases (the bathtub), an on-model corporate incidence channel with an endogenous individual-side offset, entity shifting across the C/PT boundary, income conversion into the unrealized-gain state (σ), evasion as a leak, and charitable responses. The question is answerable in a way it was not before. What remains open is how to state the answer — that is what this document designs.

2. The full object, before any pruning

Every exhibit we could produce is a projection of one object. Stating it explicitly forces every pruning decision into the open.

For a policy package x = (top ordinary rate, top CG rate, death regime, estate params, wealth-tax params, corporate rate, QBI, NIIT, …) the model produces:

  • R(x, t, h) — revenue by calendar year t and by tax head h (individual income, payroll, capital gains within IIT, estate, wealth, corporate), under two run types (static = law-only; conventional = with all behavioral/mechanical channels).
  • A mechanism account of the static−conventional wedge, decomposable by channel: realization response, entity shifting, conversion (σ), evasion, charity, bathtub financing, corporate offset. This taxonomy is model-defined, not God-given — the bathtub is mechanical in implementation but rides on a calibrated saving profile; σ is conservation (dollars resurface in later years under a different head), evasion is a leak (they don't); the corporate offset is neither. Any exhibit that aggregates these owes the reader the definitions.
  • E(x) — the incidence cube: effective tax rates in LEVELS, baseline and reform side by side, by income group × income definition {AGI, expanded, Haig-Simons} × ranking {fixed, self} × cumulative tax tier {IIT → +payroll → +death taxes → +wealth/CIT/VAT} × corporate-incidence convention {equity-supernormal, capital-income, uniform-net-worth}, with component ETRs by tax type. This exists as of 2026-07-08: static/supplemental/distribution_etrs.csv per scenario.
  • Parameter dependence θ — everything above is conditional on the behavioral bundle (σ, evasion elasticities, wealth-avoidance parameters, entity-shifting semi-elasticity, s-profile, CORP_* constants, ψ). Some of these are calibrated, some asserted, some placeholders (§6).

Dimensions any exhibit must either display or knowingly integrate out:

  1. Intensity. Interactions are curve shifts, not scalars. The effect of step-up repeal on CG revenue depends on where on the CG rate schedule you evaluate it.
  2. Horizon. The bathtub compounds; estate and wealth interactions grow with the window. Year-10 and year-30 interaction structures differ, and the divergence is a finding (“wealth taxes look better in the window than in the steady state”), not a nuisance. Both horizons, everywhere.
  3. Tax head. A wealth tax doesn't just “lose revenue to the estate-tax overlap” — it collects part of the estate base early, moves IIT receipts across decades. Cross-head accounting is richer and more honest than a leakage number.
  4. Mechanism. Mechanical base erosion ≠ avoidance re-routing ≠ evasion leak. Conflating them flattens the paper's actual contribution.
  5. Order/attribution. With the endogenous corporate offset, stacked attribution is not order-invariant. Package totals against a common baseline are the only convention-free numbers; any decomposition of a package into lever contributions requires a stated convention.
  6. Incidence. Revenue and effective rates are dual: revenue = Σ ETR × income across the distribution. A capacity claim in dollars has an exact restatement in delivered effective rates on top economic income.
  7. Parameter uncertainty. Every number is conditional on θ. Bands, not points, wherever θ is soft.

4. The deliverable: report sections as explicit projections

Audience: informed general public for the report body; an interactive layer for practitioners. Every section below states what it shows and what it integrates out.

§A. Standalone revenue schedules (the greenbook layer)

Each instrument alone, at 3–5 intensities, both horizons, conventional and static. ~30–40 runs, reusable beyond this project.

Integrates out: interactions (by construction — that's its job as the reference surface). Discloses: the θ-bundle in force.

§B. Conditional revenue schedules (the interaction layer)

The generalization of “CG raises more without step-up” that keeps intensity structure: for each economically motivated ordered pair (i | j), the full revenue schedule of instrument i re-run with j at central intensity — plotted against the §A standalone schedule. The reader sees the whole curve shift and where it bends, not a scalar. Candidate pairs (~10–14, chosen for economic content, not factorial completeness):

  • CG rate | death regime (the crown-jewel pair — the realization elasticity is policy-dependent and we derive it)
  • CG rate | corporate rate (upstream base erosion)
  • Top ordinary | CG + death regime (the σ wedge: closing the capital-side exit raises the ordinary-rate yield)
  • Estate | wealth tax (same stock, death vs. annual timing)
  • Wealth tax | CG rate (bathtub + reported-base interactions)
  • Estate | CG-at-death treatment (deemed realization cannibalizes the estate base)
  • Corporate | top ordinary (entity-shifting boundary)
  • QBI | top ordinary

A summary interaction matrix (cell = % change in i's 10-yr yield at central intensity when j is on) goes to the appendix, with its convention stated — it is a compression of §B, not a headline.

Integrates out: higher-order interactions, horizon in the matrix cells (fix by showing 10y and 30y matrices). AUTHOR CALL the pair list.

§C. Coherent packages and cross-head accounting

Not a 5,500-run factorial. Three to five named, internally coherent packages at escalating ambition — e.g. Rates-only (push statutory rates, change no structure), Base-first (step-up repeal, QBI, NIIT before rate hikes), Full-stack (rates + structure + estate/wealth), possibly a Rates-only-pushed-to-match variant that hits Full-stack's revenue on rates alone (if it can at all — its failure is the finding). For each package:

  1. Package total vs. sum of standalone parts, both horizons — the overstatement quantified, in dollars and percent.
  2. Revenue by tax head by year — where the dollars actually arrive, under which statute, in which decade. A stacked-area exhibit per package. This replaces the Sankey: it is the same information without inventing a leakage taxonomy for the headline.
  3. Mechanism decomposition of the static−conventional wedge by channel, with definitions, as a table (not a ribbon chart).

Attribution of the parts-vs-whole gap to specific pairs: AUTHOR CALL between (a) pairwise-from-§B + explicit “higher-order + ordering” residual (cheap, honest, residual can be large) and (b) Shapley over levers (order-invariant and complete; 2k runs per package — feasible for k≤8 at ~256 runs/package, but only for a subset of packages). Default: (a) for all packages, (b) once, for the Full-stack package, as a check on the residual.

§D. The effective-rate statement (the new ETR work — the centerpiece candidate)

Revenue totals answer “how much money”; they are horizon-tangled and attribution-dependent. The dual question — what effective tax rate does each package actually deliver on the true economic income of the top, and how far short of the statutory push does it land? — is cleaner, and as of this week the model answers it natively. distribution_etrs.csv gives, for every scenario: ETR levels (baseline and reform side by side) by income group, under a Haig-Simons income definition (accrued gains in the denominator — the concept behind the “billionaires pay 8%” genre of claims, now computable under counterfactual policy), with the corporate tax allocated under three conventions and taxes accumulated in tiers (IIT → +payroll → +death → +wealth/CIT/VAT) with component ETRs by tax type.

Proposed exhibits:

  1. Delivered-vs-statutory: for each package (and along one or two §B intensity ladders), the top-0.1%/top-1% Haig-Simons ETR, baseline vs. reform, against the statutory top rates the package legislates. The vertical gap between “what the law says” and “what lands on economic income” — and how the structure levers (death regime, QBI, NIIT) close it while pure rate levers mostly don't — is the paper's thesis in incidence units, with no leakage taxonomy required.
  2. ETR by percentile, current law vs. named packages, at the all-taxes-in tier, HS definition, with the three corporate conventions as a visible robustness band rather than a footnote. (We already know the conventions matter: under income ranking, the capital-income convention puts ~50% of the corporate tax on the top 1% vs ~29% under equity-supernormal — that spread IS content for this audience.)
  3. Component stack at the top: for the top 0.1%, the component-ETR decomposition (IIT / payroll / death / wealth / corporate) under each package — the incidence-side mirror of §C's revenue-by-head.

One honest limitation to resolve: the ETR file is static-sourced (house convention D20 — distribution is a static concept). So the ETRs show the law-only incidence; behavioral re-routing (conversion, evasion, bathtub erosion) does not shrink the numerator. For THIS project the gap between the statutory-incidence ETR and the behavior-inclusive delivered rate is close to the point. AUTHOR CALL build a conventional-side ETR variant (same cube measured on conventional detail — machinery exists, convention change only) so §D can show statutory rate → static ETR → delivered-after-behavior ETR as a two-stage shortfall; or keep D20 strictly and state the limitation. Default: build the variant, label it clearly as a departure from the distribution convention, report both.

Integrates out: year-by-year timing (ETR snapshots at 2–3 display years); within-group heterogeneity. Gate: corp-alloc placeholder exposures (§6).

§E. Robustness and uncertainty

  • θ-bands on headline numbers: σ, evasion top-multiplier, wealth-avoidance parameters, s-profile, CORP_* corners. Bands enter as ranges on the §C/§D headline exhibits, not as a separate 40-page appendix.
  • Migration/expatriation: off-model haircut band on wealth/estate-lever yields (the critics' first objection; a module is not warranted for v1).
  • The σ-conditionality statement: σ = 0.08 is a residual conditional on the rest of the stack; re-derive if any stack component changes (guard exists in code).
  • Full parameter table: every behavioral parameter, value, provenance, status (calibrated / asserted / placeholder). This is where the archived codex review's “citation crosswalk” requirement lands, once, before publication.

§F. The interactive layer (for the nerds)

Serves the object, not a toy: pick a package → see its §C revenue-by-head time paths, its parts-vs-whole comparison, its §D ETR profile, at both horizons, with the θ-band. The run set behind it is §A + §B + §C (order ~100–200 runs, not 5,500). A slider-per-lever full-factorial explorer is explicitly out of scope for v1 — exact runs only, over the exhibits' actual support. AUTHOR CALL whether the explorer ships at all in v1 or the report links run-level CSVs.

5. What §4 deliberately discards (the pruning, stated)

  • Full-factorial coverage (the archived ~5,500-run campaign): replaced by economically chosen pairs + named packages. We lose the ability to quote arbitrary 4-lever combinations; we keep everything the exhibits actually show, exactly.
  • Revenue-maximization / frontier framing: stays dropped (2026-07-07 ruling stands). “Capacity” is bounded by the delivered-ETR and parts-vs-whole exhibits, not by a Laffer argmax.
  • Low/central/high elasticity bundles as separate full grids: replaced by θ-bands on headline numbers (targeted re-runs, not grid duplication).
  • A single leakage-taxonomy headline chart (Sankey): replaced by revenue-by-head accounting (§C) + mechanism table with definitions.
